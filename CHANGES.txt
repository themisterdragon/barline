BARLINE CHANGES

NEW IN 0.6.0
  - End: the loops stop with the ending's last hit (the final crash), not a bar after it. In
    Multi and Sync both tracks stop.
  - Record A -> B: one press (f, or the iPad button under Record) records A for its length, B the
    instant A closes, then A plays when B closes. No waiting between sections. For new loops.
  - Your own router plugged into the laptop's USB port: the iPad page shows its address (Network:
    Auto picks a USB router first, or pick USB router / Wi-Fi / Cable), an option lets only that
    network in, and barline doctor helps get the router's link an address. A change from the
    iPad that would shut that iPad out is refused. The T2 Mac's own chip link is ignored.
  - Windows and macOS: Barline runs on Windows 10/11 (WASAPI sound, Windows MIDI; double-click
    barline.exe for the window) and macOS 11+ (Core Audio, Core MIDI; Barline.app), as well as
    Linux. Same screens, same iPad page, same timing engine.
  - A new look for the laptop screen: big tempo digits, beat lights with the bar sweeping under
    them, A (blue) and B (violet) loop tiles like the iPad's, a clean "lately" list, and keycaps
    along the bottom grouped Drums / Loops / More. Click any keycap or loop tile with the mouse.
    ? shows every key by what it's for. Plain on plain terminals (NO_COLOR, the Linux console,
    no UTF-8). Every key works as before.

NEW IN 0.5.7 (bug bash; End and the clock change checked on the pedal with firmware 2.0)
  - Fixed: starting the drums for the next song made the pedal play the last loop by itself (the
    MIDI Start message does that), and then a tap on that loop layered onto it. Barline no longer
    sends Start; the pedal keeps to the beat from the clock alone (loops still land within ~5 ms of
    the bar line). midi_start = on in the settings file brings the old way back.
  - Stop Now stops the loops too, on the next bar line (a loop stopped mid-bar comes back off the
    grid); the drums still stop at once.
  - A switch waiting for its bar line can be taken back: Stop Loops, End, or tapping the loop
    that's playing cancels it. Tapping A then Next Part at once no longer presses A twice (which
    layered onto it).
  - Next Part only switches to a track Barline knows has a loop (a press on an empty track starts
    a recording).
  - A press that has started always gets its let-go (a held note is a long press: Undo or Clear).

NEW IN 0.5.6 (Song mode checked on the pedal with firmware 2.0: every switch on the bar line)
  - Song mode: tap A or B and that loop comes in on the next bar line. Next Part switches the loop
    too, with the new part. End stops the loop with the drums' ending.
  - Fixed: picking a track sent "Select Track" to the pedal, which in Song mode stopped the loop
    that was playing without starting the other (silence), and then Record/Stop/Clear meant for A
    could land on B. Each press now picks its own track a moment before it goes.
  - A new Play page: what's connected and the pedal mode at the top, the part name big enough to
    read from the mic, A (blue) and B (violet) as big tiles that say Playing / Next bar /
    Recording / Ready / Empty, one Record button ("Layer on" when the track has a loop), one Pause.
    A tile says NEXT BAR the moment you tap it. Phones on their side get two columns, Android
    phones tick under your thumb, and the page honors Reduce Motion.
  - The bottom bar on other tabs has A and B too, so you can switch loops from the Lyrics tab.
  - Pedal mode moved to Settings > The pedal. Verse / Chorus buttons and Follow the song are gone
    (Next Part does both).

NEW IN 0.5.5 (tested on the pedal with firmware 2.0, with a one-hit test loop)
  - Play A / Play B bring a loop back exactly on the bar line, every time (within 2 ms in testing):
    a loop Barline stopped (Stop Loops stops on the bar line) comes straight back in on the next
    bar; a loop that's playing stops on the next bar line and comes back in on the one after (the
    pedal can't take a Stop and a Play at once). Works on loops you recorded yourself too.
  - Pause 1/2 bar always waits for the next measure (it could cut into the bar you were in).
  - Picking track A or B waits for the next bar line (in Song mode it switches loops).
  - Good to know: the pedal picks a stopped loop up from where it stopped. Stopped with your foot in
    the middle of a bar, it comes back off the grid; stop with Barline (iPad Stop Loops or a
    footswitch set up in Barline) and it comes back in time.

NEW IN 0.5.4
  - Song mode loops can be different lengths: Loop length sets the selected track, so the verse
    on A can be 4 bars and the chorus on B 2. Songs save both (Track B length in the song editor;
    blank = the same as A).
  - Barline sets the pedal's mode again (pedal firmware 1.0.1 and later take mode changes over
    MIDI; checked on firmware 2.0.0). On firmware 1.0.0, set the mode on the pedal too.

NEW IN 0.5.3
  - Song mode from the iPad: Play A / Play B switch loops on the next bar line, and "Follow the
    song" plays loop A in the verse and loop B in the chorus, switching where the fill ends.
  - Pedal mode (Single, Multi, Sync, Song, or Not sure): tell Barline the mode the pedal is set
    to (on the pedal: Menu > Mode; it doesn't take mode changes over MIDI), and it presses track B
    the right way for it (the B notes work in Sync and Song; Multi selects B first).
  - Track A / B buttons show which tracks have a loop.
  - The check-up (barline doctor) and the Guide point at the pedal's Ext Ctrl setting: when
    it's off, the pedal ignores every press.

NEW IN 0.5.2
  - Fixed: loops could come out a beat off. A late Record press started the loop on the next
    beat (the "grace" from 0.3.0), which cut off the one and recorded the next bar's one instead;
    and a press too close to a bar line could reach the pedal just after its downbeat, so the
    pedal started a beat late. Now every loop starts and ends on a bar line, with the pedal's
    press always early enough, and the screen counts down to the bar it starts on.
  - Tracks A and B: Record on B while A is recording starts B the moment A closes (on that bar
    line) instead of confusing the pedal; Record again on the same track closes it early on the
    next bar line. The one-button footswitch keeps track of A and B separately.

NEW IN 0.5.1
  - Dramatic pause: Pause 1 bar (the whole next bar) or Pause 1/2 bar (the second half of a bar).
    The drums stop dead, then come back on the one with a crash; the bars and clock keep going.
  - Clap along (on/off): the drums drop out and hand claps play on the backbeat until you tap it
    again; then the drums come back on the next bar with a crash. Kits without claps borrow the
    Cajon kit's real ones (or Barline's own).
    On the iPad: Play tab and the bottom bar. Laptop: z / x pause half / whole bar, d clap along.
  - The beat editor (iPad and laptop) edits every part: both fills (into the chorus and back to
    the verse, now separate), the count-in, the ending, and the clap-along pattern. Two-bar fills
    and endings play both bars now. Play repeats the part you're editing, a transition after a
    bar of its groove.
  - Fixed: a sound device another program was using could make Barline wait forever (and
    hang when quitting) instead of saying it's busy and trying again.

NEW IN 0.5.0 (ready for the stage)
  - A window of its own: Barline in the app menu (or barline window) opens everything in a
    window on the laptop, no terminal needed and no pairing code. Closing it quits Barline;
    opening it again while it runs brings the window back. In the terminal, v opens it too.
  - barline headless: no screen at all, just the iPad page (a computer at the side of the stage).
  - barline doctor: checks the pedal, sound, MIDI, permissions, the iPad page, and the window,
    and says how to fix what's off. barline doctor --fix keeps PipeWire off the pedal.
  - Plug and unplug: the pedal can be plugged in after Barline starts, or come back after a
    loose cable mid-song; Barline picks it up within a couple of seconds and keeps the beat.
    (Before, an unplugged pedal made the bars race ahead.)
  - The laptop's own sound is only used while the click or Hear on laptop is on, so the computer's
    other sound works the rest of the time, and turning them on never interrupts the drums.
  - Built-in sound that only takes 4 or 6 channels, or odd block sizes (like a T2 Mac's), opens now
    (the sound goes to the first two channels).
  - One Barline at a time; a second one just brings up the first one's window.
  - A clean quit when the terminal closes or the computer shuts down (the pedal's clock is stopped).
  - barline.log keeps what happened (for "what went wrong at the gig?").
  - Runs on ARM too (Raspberry Pi 4/5): the package has both programs and the installer picks.
  - The installer adds Barline to the app menu; uninstall.sh removes it (keeping your songs).

  The iPad page, for the stage:
  - Lyrics (and Songs, Beats, Settings) keep a bar at the bottom with the beat lights, the bar
    and part, tempo -/+, and Start/End, Next Part, Record, Overdub, however far the lyrics scroll.
  - The count-in shows as huge numbers you can read from across the stage.
  - The beat lights move smoothly and on time, even when the Wi-Fi is slow.
  - "Lost touch with Barline" shows (in red) if the connection drops; it reconnects itself.
  - The pedal connection shows on the Play tab (and "No pedal" in the bottom bar).
  - Keep the screen on (Settings > This device).
  - Tap tempo is timed on the iPad itself, so Wi-Fi delays don't throw it off.
  - Clear and Stop Now need a short hold, so a stray tap can't wipe a loop or kill the drums.
  - Switching songs while the drums play takes a second tap.
  - A Bluetooth footswitch or keyboard on the iPad can work like the laptop's keys, scroll the
    lyrics, or do nothing.
  - Section names in lyrics (Verse, [Chorus], Bridge:) stand out; each song remembers its
    auto-scroll speed; auto-scroll can start with the drums.
  - On an iPad on its side (or the laptop window) the Play tab is two columns.

NEW IN 0.4.0
  - Import from a set list app: in the app, export its database
    (to Files / iCloud Drive). Then on the Barline iPad page: Songs tab > Import, choose the
    .sqlite file. Every song and its lyrics come in, and every show becomes a set list (in
    order). New songs start on the Singer-Songwriter beat at 100 BPM: pick a beat and tempo for
    each (Edit), or set the feel while it plays and tap "Save tempo, beat & feel to this song".
    Importing again later updates the songs and keeps the beats you picked.

NEW IN 0.3.1
  - Feel mode: g switches between LOCKED to the grid (every hit exactly on time) and Human
    (your pocket and looseness). Switching never loses your settings; nudging the pocket or
    looseness while locked switches back to Human. On the iPad: the two buttons on the Feel card.
    Each song remembers its mode ("Save tempo, beat & feel to this song").

NEW IN 0.3.0 (more forgiving)
  - Late Record/Overdub: press within about a beat after the bar line and it starts on the next
    beat instead of waiting a whole bar. The loop is still exactly the length you picked, so it
    stays in time with the drums. (grace_beats in the settings file; 0 turns it off.)
  - Feel: w swing (the beat's own, 0, 25, 50, 66%), < > pocket (pushed / laid back; the snare
    and hats move, the kick stays on the beat), ( ) looseness. On the iPad: the Feel card on the
    Play tab, with "Save tempo, beat & feel to this song".
  - Tempo changes glide over about a beat instead of jumping (a loop being recorded still comes
    out exactly its length). If loops are already playing on the pedal you get a heads-up: they
    can't follow a tempo change.

NEW IN 0.2.1
  - Drums go to the pedal ONLY. The laptop's own speakers/headphones get them only when you
    turn on Hear on laptop (h, or the button on the iPad's Beats tab). It's off every time
    Barline starts. The beat builder turns it on when you play a beat there, and turns it
    (and the preview) off when you leave. The click never goes to the pedal.

NEW IN 0.2.0
  - A clean stop: when the drums end (or the loop you recorded closes), the clock stops and
    the bars reset. Starting again counts in fresh.
  - 25 signature grooves (Heartland Jangle, Swung Strum-Along, Loud-Quiet-Loud, Seven Four in
    7/4...). p = your favorites, a = all beats; in the list, f stars a beat.
  - Beat builder: j (starts from a copy of the beat that's on). Arrows, space places a hit,
    X accent, o soft, g ghost, tab = verse/chorus/fill, m time signature, enter plays it, s saves.
  - The iPad/phone page: the address and code are at the top of the Barline screen.
    Open it in Safari on the same Wi-Fi, type the code once, then Share > Add to Home Screen.
      Play: start/end, next part, tempo/tap, big beat lights, loop buttons
      Lyrics: big lyrics for the current song, size, auto-scroll, prev/next song
      Songs: set lists (tap to load a song's beat/tempo/kit/pedal slot), edit songs and lyrics,
             import a set list app's spreadsheet (CSV)
      Beats: favorites, all beats, kits
