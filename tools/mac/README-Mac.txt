BARLINE FOR macOS (11 Big Sur or later; Apple silicon and Intel)

START
  Drag Barline.app to Applications. Double-click it: Barline opens in its own window (in Chrome,
  Edge, or Brave if you have one; otherwise Safari). Closing that window quits Barline.

  The first time, macOS may say it can't check Barline for malicious software (it isn't from the
  App Store). Right-click (or Control-click) Barline.app, choose Open, then Open again. On newer
  macOS: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway. When macOS asks whether Barline may
  accept incoming network connections, say Allow, so your iPad or phone can reach it.

THE LOOPER PEDAL
  Plug it in with USB; no driver needed. On the pedal (hold the encoder for the menu):
  MIDI > Source USB, Clock On, Ext Ctrl On.

THE TERMINAL SCREEN
  In Terminal:  /Applications/Barline.app/Contents/MacOS/barline       (add "doctor" to check the
  setup). Everything else is in GUIDE.txt; where it says ~/.local/share/barline, on a Mac your
  songs and settings are in ~/Library/Application Support/Barline.
