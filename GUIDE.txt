BARLINE - drums, a click, and perfectly timed loops for a USB looper pedal

Barline plays real drum kits into your pedal, keeps the pedal in time with MIDI clock, and
presses Record/Overdub for you exactly on the bar line, so every loop comes out the right length.
You run it from a laptop (Linux, Windows, or a Mac; even an old one) and play it from the laptop's
keyboard, a page-turner footswitch, or an iPad/phone on the same Wi-Fi.


WHAT YOU NEED
  - A computer: Linux (Intel/AMD, or ARM like a Raspberry Pi 4/5), Windows 10 or 11 (Intel/AMD or
    ARM), or a Mac with macOS 11 or later (Apple silicon or Intel). Nothing else to install.
  - The looper pedal on a USB cable.
  - Optional: an iPad or phone, a page-turner footswitch, headphones for the click.


INSTALL
  Windows: unzip Barline-*-windows-*.zip, double-click barline.exe (README-Windows.txt).
  Mac: unzip Barline-*-mac.zip, drag Barline.app to Applications, open it (README-Mac.txt).
  Linux:
  tar xzf Barline-*.tar.gz
  cd Barline-*/
  sh install.sh
Then "Barline" is in your app menu. Check everything with:   barline doctor
(It says what's wrong in plain words and how to fix it.) Installing a newer version the same way
keeps your songs, beats, and settings.


SET UP THE LOOPER PEDAL (once; hold the encoder for the menu)
  MIDI > Source: USB    Clock: On    Ext Ctrl: On    Channel: Omni
  Logic: R>P>D
  Ext Ctrl matters most: when it's off, the pedal ignores everything Barline sends, and loops
  never start. With Clock off, a Record press just flashes Rec at 0:00 and waits.


THREE WAYS TO RUN IT
  Barline (app menu)  or  barline window
      A window on the laptop with everything: Play, Lyrics, Songs, Beats, Settings.
      Closing the window quits Barline. Opening it again while it runs brings the window back.
  barline        (in a terminal)
      The keyboard screen. v opens the window too.
  barline headless
      No screen at all, just the iPad page: for a computer tucked away at the side of the stage.

The iPad page and the window are the same thing. The address and a 6-digit code are on the
laptop (top of the terminal screen, or Settings in the window). Open the address in Safari on the
same Wi-Fi, type the code once, then Share > Add to Home Screen.


PLAYING
  Start           count-in (the numbers show big on the iPad), then the beat
  Next Part       a fill, then verse <-> chorus
  End             a proper ending on the next bar, then everything stops cleanly
  Stop Now        hold it: stops instantly (a stray tap can't kill the drums mid-song)
  Pause 1 bar     a dramatic pause: the drums stop for the whole next bar (cymbals grabbed),
                  then come back on the one with a crash
  Pause 1/2 bar   the same for the second half of a bar (this bar if there's time, else the next)
  Clap along      on/off: from the next bar the drums drop out and hand claps play on the
                  backbeat; tap it again and the drums come back on the next bar with a crash
  (The bars and the pedal's clock keep going through both, so your loops stay in time; the
  loops themselves keep playing.) The bottom bar has Pause and Clap too; whether its Pause is
  a whole or half bar is in Settings > This device.
  Tap             tap the tempo on the iPad itself (timed there, so Wi-Fi delays don't matter)
  Feel            Locked to the grid, or Human (swing, pocket, looseness)

The drums go to the pedal only. To hear them on the laptop (practice, no pedal), turn on
Hear on laptop. It's off every time Barline starts.

The Lyrics tab always keeps the beat lights and the Start / Next / A / B / Record / Pause buttons
at the bottom, however far the lyrics scroll. Auto-scroll speed is in lines a minute, and each
song remembers its own. In Settings, "Lyrics auto-scroll starts with the drums" does what it says.


BEATS OF YOUR OWN
  Beats tab > Edit (or j on the laptop). Every part can be changed: Verse, Chorus, Fill to
  chorus, Fill to verse, Count-in, Ending, and Clap along (fills and endings can be 1 or 2 bars;
  an empty ending just stops). Play repeats the part you're on, a fill or ending after a bar of
  its groove, so you hear the transition. Built-in beats are saved as a copy of your own.


LOOPS
  Record          the pedal starts recording on the next bar line and plays exactly N bars later.
                  On a track that already has a loop it says "Layer on": it adds a layer for one
                  loop length (the pedal's Record/Play does that on a full track). To start a
                  track over, hold Clear first.
  Loops always start and end on a bar line. Press during the bar BEFORE you start playing: the
  screen counts down ("Recording A in 3") to the one. A press on (or after) the one waits for the
  next bar, so the loop never comes out a beat off.
  Record again on the same track while it records: it closes early, on the next bar line.
  Record on the other track while one records: that one starts the moment this one closes
  (in Multi mode, one bar later).
  Track A / B: big tiles that say what each track is doing (Playing, Next bar, Recording, Ready,
  Empty). A is blue and B is violet everywhere. Tapping one aims Record at it; in Song mode it
  also switches the pedal to that loop on the next bar line (see below).
  Pedal mode (Settings > The pedal): Barline sets the pedal to it (pedal firmware 1.0.1 or later; update it with the
  maker's firmware updater on a Mac or PC). On firmware
  1.0.0, set the same mode on the pedal too (hold the encoder > Mode).
  Loop length: for the selected track. In Song mode A and B can differ (verse 4, chorus 2).

SONG MODE: A FOR THE VERSE, B FOR THE CHORUS
  Song mode keeps two loops of any length, and only one plays at a time. Record the verse loop on
  A and the chorus loop on B. Then:
  Tap A or B          that loop comes in on the next bar line (the tile says NEXT BAR until it
                      does). An empty track isn't played: the tap gets it ready to record.
  Next Part           the drums fill, and the other loop comes in with the new part.
  End                 the drums play their ending and the loops stop on its last hit (the final
                      crash), not a bar later. Multi and Sync stop both tracks.
  Stop Now (hold)     the drums stop at once; the loop stops on the next bar line (so it comes
                      back in time).
  Record A -> B (f, or the button under Record): one press records the whole song's loops with
                      no waiting in between. A records for its length from the next bar line, B
                      starts the instant A closes, and when B closes A plays. Set each track's
                      length first ([ ] with A or B picked). For new loops: clear A and B first.
  Changed your mind before the bar line? Tap the loop that's playing: the switch is called off.
  Next Part only switches to a track Barline knows has a loop (recorded or played this session).
  "Not sure" works the same way as Song.
  Keep loops in time: stop them with Barline (Stop Loops stops on the bar line). The pedal
  picks a stopped loop up where it stopped, so a foot stop mid-bar brings it back off the grid.
  Right after Barline starts it can't know what the pedal is playing (the pedal doesn't say),
  so the first switch stops on one bar and plays on the next (a Play on a loop that's already
  playing would overdub it). From then on, switches are clean.
  Why Barline never just "selects" a track on the pedal: in Song mode, selecting stops the
  playing loop without starting the other. Each press picks its own track a moment before it goes.
  Clear           hold it (so a stray tap can't wipe a loop)
  Loop length     1, 2, 4, 8, or 16 bars


SONGS, SET LISTS, LYRICS
  Songs tab: pick a set list, tap a song to load its tempo, beat, kit, and feel. While the drums
  play, switching songs takes a second tap (so a stray tap can't change the tempo mid-song).
  Edit a song (lyrics, tempo, beat, pedal slot...), or play it the way you like and tap
  "Save tempo, beat, kit & feel to this song".
  From a set list app: Export Database (to Files), then Songs tab > Import.


FOOTSWITCH / KEYBOARD (laptop)
  space drums   n next part   e ending   z/x pause half/whole bar   d clap along
  t tap   +/- tempo   r record   o overdub   f record A then B   y the look (themes, beat lights)
  Page Down: record, then overdub (one-button)   Page Up: undo   left/right: previous/next song
  u undo   c clear   s stop loops   b track A/B   [ ] loop length   h hear on laptop   ? help
On the iPad, a Bluetooth footswitch can work like the laptop's keys, scroll the lyrics, or do
nothing (Settings > This device).


IF SOMETHING'S WRONG
  barline doctor            checks the pedal, sound, MIDI, the iPad page, and the window
  Pedal unplugged mid-song?   The bars keep counting; plug it back in and Barline picks it up
                              by itself within a couple of seconds.
  iPad says "Lost touch"?     Same Wi-Fi? Is the laptop asleep? It reconnects on its own.
  No venue Wi-Fi?             Bring your own: plug a travel router into the laptop's USB port (a
                              USB network adapter to one of its LAN ports, or a router that shares
                              its network over USB) and put the iPad on its Wi-Fi. Network: Auto
                              shows the router's address first (Settings > Network on the iPad).
                              "Only devices on that network" keeps the venue's Wi-Fi out.
                              No address on the router's link? barline doctor says how to get one.
                              The firewall may need the port open (doctor says how).
  iPad screen goes to sleep?  Settings > This device > Keep the screen on, and for a gig also the
                              iPad's Settings > Display & Brightness > Auto-Lock > Never.
  "Another program is using it" for the pedal: PipeWire grabbed it. barline doctor --fix tells
                              PipeWire to leave the pedal alone (and how to undo that).
  What happened at the gig?   ~/.local/share/barline/barline.log


YOUR STUFF
  ~/.local/share/barline: songs/, setlists/, beats/, barline.conf. Plain text: copy the
  folder to back it up. Uninstall with: sh uninstall.sh (keeps your stuff).
