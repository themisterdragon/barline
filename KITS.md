# Drum kit credits

Barline's sampled kits are mixed from these open-source sample libraries (each hit is the
author's own microphone mix at its default settings, trimmed and converted to 16-bit WAV).

- **Virtuosity Studio**: Versilian Studios and Karoryfer Samples. License: CC0-1.0. Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums
- **Naked Drums**: Wilkinson Audio. License: CC-BY-4.0. Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/WilkinsonAudio.NakedDrums
- **Big Rusty**: Karoryfer Samples. License: CC0-1.0. Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.big-rusty-drums
- **Gated '80s**: Big Rusty (Karoryfer Samples) with a gated reverb added by Barline. License: CC0-1.0. Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.big-rusty-drums
- **DRS Rock**: DrumGizmo (Lars Muldjord, Bent Bisballe Nyeng). License: CC-BY-4.0. Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/DrumGizmo.DRSKit
- **Muldjord**: DrumGizmo (Lars Muldjord). License: CC-BY-4.0. Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/DrumGizmo.MuldjordKit
- **Swirly Brushes**: Karoryfer Samples. License: CC0-1.0. Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.swirly-drums
- **Gogodze Phu**: Karoryfer Samples. License: CC0-1.0. Source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.gogodze-phu-vol-ii
- **Cajon & Shakers**: Versilian Studios (VCSL). License: CC0-1.0. Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL

CC-BY-4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ (the samples were changed: mixed, trimmed, converted).
